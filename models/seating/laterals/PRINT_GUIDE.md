# Side pads Round 5: print guide (P1S, PETG)

Use the STLs named by part number (`GBG-SP-<code>-<variant>-r5.stl`), exported from the Onshape Part Studio **"Side pads r5 - kit (print layout + coupon)"**, where every part is already in its print orientation.

| Part | Orientation | Supports | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR carrier | on the nose's outer face (button pocket on the bed) | tree support under the arm bend only | tongue and tooth channel lie in the layer plane |
| BT button-catch | standing, as fitted (block/tooth top on the bed) | none; the cap's round end touches the bed | tooth root bends in the layer plane |
| TS tell sleeve | flat ring | none | orange PETG |
| DP depth piece | foot face down (tilted feet: the tilted face down) | none | dovetail flanks at 60° print clean; the nut pockets open on the bed side |
| PD pad shell | child side down | tree supports on the child side only | |
| RG clip ring | flat side down, or print flat and bend in | tree supports if printed curved | |
| CP blank cap | outer face down | none | |
| WG wing reprint | as the chair's wing; check the notch's bridged ceiling in the slicer | slicer supports | |

**Slicer modifiers (internal strength, no extra parts):** use 6 perimeters and 100 % infill in modifier volumes at
- the button's tooth and block (the 0.4 hidden slits add internal walls on their own),
- the carrier's tongue root (first 8 mm behind the nose's back face) and the arm root,
- the wing's groove boss (the outer wall around the catch groove, z 223-247).

**Assembly order:** bond the Ø6 x 35 rod and the 4 Ø2 x 21 pins with high-Tg epoxy. Drop the spring into its slot. Press the button-catch and slide it down from the nose's top face into its channels. Snap the orange sleeve in under the lip. Drop the two M5 nuts into the foot's grooves. Slide the pad's rail into a groove from the back, then fit the M5 x 10 from the child side. Put the depth piece on the tenon and fit the M5 x 12. Fit the foam and the cover; press the clip ring in until the barb clicks.

**Fits to check on the coupon first:** sleeve snap (0.3/side), the latch click, the wedge seating (keep > 1°/side), crush ribs, and the 2.4 mm skin behind the groove.

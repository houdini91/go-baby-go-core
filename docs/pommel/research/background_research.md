# Pommel research summary (2026-11-10, background research agent; sources listed with URLs)

## Clinical
- A pommel must NOT stop forward sliding; "the groin is not designed to be load-bearing". Sliding is handled by the pelvic belt (anchored in front of the trochanters, 60–80° for posterior tilt) plus posterior pelvic support. hiaus.net.au/knowledge-hub/pommels-what-they-are-and-what-they-arent ; thiis.co.uk/?p=19530 ; Leckey pelvic belt research PDF.
- ISO 7176-26 calls it a "medial knee support". It sits at the distal thigh / knee / upper shin and must be "installed clear of the groin" (no mm figure published). Natural femur position is about 5° abduction.
- Removal for transfers: Velcro (Leckey), quick release (Etac/R82), swing-down (Permobil).

## Safety
- Rigid core plus soft or wipeable cover (Etac, Permobil). PETG isn't certified for skin contact, so cover it or pad it.
- Finger-entrapment analog (ASTM F1004/F406): if a hole admits a 5.33 mm rod, it must also admit 9.53 mm. → counterbores ≥ 10 mm, or < 5.3 mm.
- No moving parts means no pinch points.

# Pommel v0.7 — design resources (PRE-FIRST-PRINT)

Supporting material for [`models/seating/pommel`](../../models/seating/pommel/). Open the HTML pages in a browser.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `body_measurements_reference.html` | Why the pommel is low over the screws. Side and top sketches of a small child (2 y, 5th percentile) and a big child (5 y, 95th percentile) on the chair, using the real pommel outlines, plus a measurement table and the groin keep-low rule. |
| `design_v07b_sketch.html` | The approved v0.7b design sketch: saddle and hook, the short and long pommels, the rod layout and the neck cross-section. Some details changed in CAD: the rods are now held by crush ribs rather than a pinch bolt, and the recess is now 6 mm. |
| `research/panel_review.md` | Expert-panel review (seating PT/OT, anthropometrics, engineering). It covers the knee positions, the width/height spec, the groin clearance rule and the load cases. It was written about the earlier v0.5/v0.6 designs; its spec drove v0.7. |
| `research/background_research.md` | Clinical guidance, commercial pommel sizes and child anthropometric tables (NIST AnthroKids 1977), with sources. |
| `research/anthro_calc.py` | Knee-gap and knee-position calculation used by the panel. |
| `tools/pommel_body_page.py` | Regenerates `body_measurements_reference.html`. |

All measurements are estimates from typically developing children. Children in the programme are often smaller. Confirm the fit with a PT/OT.

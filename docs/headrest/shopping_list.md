**How much each covers.** One 500 mm rod gives the pair for any one height (85 + H mm each), or two pairs (H0 + H75, or H25 + H50); buy a second only for more headrests. The screw packs (50 / 50 / 20) and nut packs (50 / 30) cover several headrests.

**Not needed any more (vs the v1.0 padding kit):** the punch set. v1.1 has no screwdriver holes in the foam.

**Carbon cutting kit (skip what you already have).** Carbon dust irritates the lungs, skin and eyes. Cut wet or over a damp cloth, then wipe up or vacuum. **Never use a blower** (it spreads the dust). Seal each cut end with a drop of thin CA: caps would make the rod thicker than its Ø8.3 bore.

Also used, usually already at hand: masking tape (wrap the cut line), nitrile gloves, 240–400 grit sandpaper (deburr the ends), a damp cloth or spray bottle, and a small clamp (any hardware store) to hold the printed **rod cutting gauge** on the table.

**Pommel (long sizes K23/K45), same seller as the 8 mm rod:** solid carbon rod **6x500mm 1pcs** from the same listing (#3). Each long pommel needs 2 × 155 mm (K23) or 2 × 200 mm (K45), so one rod per pommel. The printed gauge is for the 8 mm headrest rods; mark the pommel rods with tape and a ruler (±1.5 mm).

**Only if you don't already have them:**
- Ø3.2 mm drill bit (to drill the PVC rod through the pod's clamp holes) and an 8 mm drill bit (to ream the rod bores if tight). Any HSS set.
- Heat gun (only to heat-form the foam roll), knife with 18 mm blades.
- PETG filament: about 380 g per headrest plus about 90 g for the fit-test bar and the template part.

**Kit parts used as-is:** the 20 mm PVC rod and the head switch come from the GoBabyGo kit.

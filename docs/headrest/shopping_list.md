**How much each covers.** One 500 mm rod gives the pair for any one height (85 + H mm each), or two pairs (H0 + H75, or H25 + H50); buy a second only for more headrests. The screw and nut packs cover several headrests.

**Per headrest (v1.2).** With one pod and one blank: 2× M5×30 + 2 M5 nuts, 2× M3×25, 2× M3×20, 1× M3×12, **5 M3 nuts**, 3× ST3.5×6.5. With a pod on both sides: 4× M3×20 and no M3×12, **6 M3 nuts**, 6× ST3.5×6.5. The rod cutting gauge (a workshop tool, printed once) takes 2× M3×30 + 2 M3 nuts. Nothing else in the headrest uses M3×30 or M3×16 any more (v1.1 used M3×30 for the chin locks and the PVC clamp, M3×16 for the pod and blank).

**Not needed any more (vs the v1.0 padding kit):** the punch set. v1.1 has no screwdriver holes in the foam.

**Carbon cutting kit (skip what you already have).** Carbon dust irritates the lungs, skin and eyes. Cut wet or over a damp cloth, then wipe up or vacuum. **Never use a blower** (it spreads the dust). Seal each cut end with a drop of thin CA: caps would make the rod thicker than its Ø8.5 bore.

Also used, usually already at hand: masking tape (wrap the cut line), nitrile gloves, 240–400 grit sandpaper (deburr the ends), a damp cloth or spray bottle, and a small clamp (any hardware store) to hold the printed **rod cutting gauge** on the table.

**Pommel (long sizes K23/K45), same seller as the 8 mm rod:** solid carbon rod **6x500mm 1pcs** from the same listing (#3). Each long pommel needs 2 × 155 mm (K23) or 2 × 200 mm (K45), so one rod per pommel. Cut them in the **Ø6 lane of the printed rod cutting gauge v2** (slots PM-6 155 and PM-6 200; the Ø2 lane has the side-pad tenon pins SP-2 29, the only side-pad carbon).

**Only if you don't already have them:**
- Ø2.5 mm drill bit (pilot holes in the PVC rod through the pod's 3 lock holes, ~5 mm deep: see the print guide) and an 8 mm drill bit (to ream the rod bores if tight). Any HSS set.
- Heat gun (only to heat-form the foam roll), knife with 18 mm blades.
- PETG filament: about 380 g per headrest plus about 90 g for the fit-test bar and the template part.

**Kit parts used as-is:** the 20 mm PVC rod and the head switch come from the GoBabyGo kit.
